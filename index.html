Tournament Directives & Clarifications

Frequently Asked Questions

Yes, absolutely! AI tools are actively encouraged during the build & practice phase to write, debug, simulate, and optimize code. However, the resulting bot file must run fully standalone and cannot make live outbound HTTP or API calls during the match itself.
Students from Keshav Memorial Engineering College (KMEC) and Neil Gogte Institute of Technology (NGIT) enrolled in CSE or CSM (AI & ML) branches across I, II, or III Year. Teams can be formed freely across branches and years.
There are strictly no draws. The strategy game engine embeds built-in deterministic tiebreaker criteria (e.g., lower average move response time, territory capture efficiency, and penalty count) to guarantee an immediate winner.
Each team must bring at least one or two laptops with Python installed, chargers, and valid college ID cards. High-speed campus Wi-Fi and power strip stations are provided across PS Rooms.
If a bot takes longer than the allotted tick budget (e.g., 80 milliseconds per move) or raises an unhandled exception, a default fallback move is chosen by the engine. Repeated crashes result in match forfeiture to ensure fairness.
